Guillermo Benito Calviño

PhD student · Inria & INSA Lyon

Hi! I'm a physicist doing a PhD in computer science at Inria and INSA Lyon, supervised by Ahmed Boubrima and Hervé Rivano.

I work on active gas source localization with multi-agent sensing systems, combining models of gas dispersion, probabilistic inference and information theory. The application is finding victims in search and rescue missions using networks of drones.

More broadly, I'm interested in statistical physics, stochastic processes and the mathematical modelling of natural phenomena and complex systems.

Research interests

  • Statistical physics and stochastic processes
  • Bayesian inference and information theory
  • Multi-agent sensing and source localization
  • Modelling of complex systems
